PROCESSING MODEL

How the engine works

  1. Read raw scanner rows.
  2. Normalize dates, times and employee IDs.
  3. Group records by employee + day.
  4. Take first / last fingerprint only.
  5. Apply schedule and overtime rules.
  6. Flag cases that require your decision.

DATA SAFETY

Original data stays untouched

The application separates raw fingerprints from calculated attendance and from accountant decisions. This keeps corrections auditable and prevents a manual decision from rewriting the original scanner data.
